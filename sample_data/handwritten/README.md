# Handwritten samples

Real handwriting on real forms.

- `herbarium_label_handwritten_glenny.pdf`, `herbarium_label_handwritten_lee.pdf`: filled plant-specimen labels (stamps, cross-outs,
  cursive and print). Source: Wikimedia Commons, "GlennyD.tif" (David Glenny) and "LeeWG.tif" (William Lee), CC BY 4.0, from the
  Allan Herbarium handwriting-samples set. Converted from TIFF to PDF at reduced size.
- `../ektachrome_notes.pdf` (in the sample_data root): a two-page handwritten photographic-developer note.

No handwritten *medical* form of reasonable quality turned up in public-domain sources; that gap is the main thing still to find.
